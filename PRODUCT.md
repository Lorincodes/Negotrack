# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lucide React, Recharts, Supabase, Resend, and Playwright. The deployment target is a standard Node.js process hosted through Namecheap cPanel Node.js App; edge-only runtime dependencies are excluded.

## Users

Small-business owners and agency partners in the United Kingdom and Spain who need to understand the health of a business's digital presence without reading a technical audit. The primary user is time-constrained and wants a clear answer to what matters, what to improve first, and whether changes are working.

## Product Purpose

NegoTrack brings website health, SEO foundations, speed, mobile usability, trust signals, conversion opportunities, reviews, local visibility, and competitor signals into one understandable view. It translates those signals into prioritised actions and tracks improvement over time. The launch website's primary outcome is a consent-based waiting-list registration while the product remains in development.

## Positioning

NegoTrack is an AI-powered business-health and digital-growth platform, not negotiation or sales software. Its distinctive mechanism is turning scattered business signals into an ordered, plain-language action plan for small-business owners. “Nego” comes from the Spanish “negocio”; “Track” means monitoring, understanding, and improving performance.

## Operating Context

The first markets are the United Kingdom and Spain. The marketing experience and metadata support `en-GB` and `es-ES`. All product interfaces shown before launch use clearly labelled demonstration data dated in 2026. The product is currently in development and private beta is coming soon.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Marketing homepage with coded, responsive product previews and simulated interactions.
- Simulated website scan only; no crawler, AI-analysis engine, or real product backend is included yet.
- Waiting-list registrations require email, country, preferred language, and privacy consent. Marketing consent is optional.
- Server-side validation, email normalisation, duplicate protection, safe URL handling, basic abuse controls, Supabase persistence, and non-blocking Resend confirmation are required.
- No fake customers, testimonials, ratings, usage figures, social links, or operational feature claims.
- No sales-negotiation, deal-pipeline, revenue-forecasting, sales-coaching, or contract-negotiation content.
- Essential content and controls remain usable with reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and assistive technology.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the product, its value, and the next action within five seconds.
2. Translate technical signals into plain-language priorities rather than exposing jargon.
3. Demonstrate the product honestly with labelled synthetic data.
4. Treat English and Spanish as first-class launch experiences.
5. Make every interaction purposeful, accessible, and fast on small-business devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices: semantic structure, labelled controls, keyboard-complete interactions, visible focus, adequate contrast, accessible form feedback, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from 375px upward. Language changes must update the document language and preserve equivalent content in English and Spanish.
