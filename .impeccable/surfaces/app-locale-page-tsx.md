## Scope and mode

The bilingual NegoTrack marketing homepage at `app/[locale]/page.tsx` is a Persuade surface. Its job is to make the product understandable within five seconds, demonstrate the planned experience honestly, and convert qualified UK and Spanish small-business owners to the private-beta waitlist.

## Audience, action, proof, and constraints

Visitors need plain-language clarity about what is holding their digital presence back and what to improve first. The primary action is a consent-based waitlist registration. Proof comes from clearly labelled coded product previews, the collect → understand → prioritise → improve workflow, bilingual/local-market coverage, and truthful launch-status statements. No customers, usage figures, testimonials, pricing, live scan capability, or operational feature claims may be invented.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Reference-led daylight product narrative: midnight navy display type, mint/teal operative signals, blue/violet analytics, hairline structure, and soft elevated product canvases. The memorable first viewport is a decisive split between the positioning statement and a large coded Business Overview console; the simulated scan later turns an entered URL into a visible staged action plan.
